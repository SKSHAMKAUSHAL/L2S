Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

L2S ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your personal data when you use our services.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and hashed password credentials for user authentication.
  • Uploaded Media: Video and audio files that you upload or provide for transcription, captioning, and vertical short clip generation.
  • OAuth Credentials: If you connect your YouTube account, we store encrypted OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) to enable video uploads to your channel.

3. YouTube API Services & Google Data

L2S uses YouTube API Services to allow you to upload generated video clips to your YouTube channel.

  • By connecting your YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for information on how Google collects and uses your data.
  • We access only the authorized Google scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, openid, email, and profile. We do not access, modify, or delete your existing videos or read your channel comments or private analytics.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. We never sell, lease, or share your Google user data with any third party.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Source Videos: Retained for up to 3 days to allow processing and clip extraction, then automatically deleted.
  • Rendered Clips: Retained for 14 days, after which they are automatically pruned.
  • Revoking Access & Deleting Tokens: You can disconnect your YouTube channel at any time within your account dashboard, which revokes and permanently deletes your stored tokens. You may also revoke L2S's access at any time via the Google Security Settings page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, contact us at support@example.com.
